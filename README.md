Vice Cover Studio — a static, offline web app that turns your own photos and a caption into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art. Pick one of four panel layouts (hero, duo split, hero + two, four panels), place up to six of your photos — one photo can fill every panel, each cropped on its own — drag to pan and scroll to zoom each panel, choose a sunset, neon or dusk colour grade, edit the caption and tagline, and export a PNG in 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 or 1920 x 1080. Everything runs in your browser from the files in `app/`; photos never leave your machine. The lettering is a bold system font with a painted gradient, outline and shadow — no Rockstar logo, artwork or font is used, and the two sample photos were made with an image model and are free to use.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — add a photo (or try a sample), arrange the panels, and hit Export PNG.
